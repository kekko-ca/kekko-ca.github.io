My card isn't translated

Translations download per language the first time you select it. Pick the language, give it a moment on Wi-Fi or cellular, and it's cached on the device afterward — including offline.

My profiles don't appear on a new device

Make sure the device is signed into the same iCloud account and iCloud Drive is on, then open kekko and wait a minute. You can also move a profile directly: in the editor, tap ••• → Share Profile to export a .kekko file, AirDrop it, and open it on the other device.

The QR code

Any phone camera works — no app needed. The card opens as a web page at kekko.ca in the scanner's browser; the data travels inside the link itself and is never stored on our site.

Deleting your data

Delete items, drink orders, or whole profiles in the app (Edit → ••• → Delete Profile). Deleting the app removes its local data; your iCloud copy can be removed in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
