Privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026

kekko is built so that we never see your data. We run no servers, require no account, and collect nothing.

What the app stores, and where

  • On your device. Your profiles — names, photos, dietary items, drink orders, and their translations — are stored locally in the app.
  • In your personal iCloud. If you are signed into iCloud, the same data syncs through your private iCloud database so it appears on your other devices. Only your Apple ID can access it; we cannot. iCloud is operated by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected."

Translation

Translations are produced on your device by Apple's Translation framework. Your dietary information is not sent to us or to any third-party translation service by kekko.

Sharing features

Sharing is always something you start, and the data goes only where you send it:

  • QR codes and card links. The card's content is compressed into the link itself. Our website (kekko.ca) decodes it entirely in the scanner's browser; the card data is in the link's fragment, which browsers do not send to the web server. We do not log or store card contents.
  • Images, printing, and .kekko files. These are created on your device and shared through the apps you choose.

Children

kekko does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children; there is nothing for it to collect.

Changes

If the app's data practices ever change, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated first, before any such version ships.
